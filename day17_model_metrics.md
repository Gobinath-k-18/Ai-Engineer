# Day 17 - House Price Prediction

## Model
Linear Regression

## Dataset
House price dataset with:
- Area
- Bedrooms
- Age
- Price

## Model Metrics

- MAE: 2.69 lakhs
- MSE: 9.53
- RMSE: 3.09 lakhs
- R²: 0.994

## Interpretation

The model's average prediction error is about 2.69 lakhs based on MAE.

The RMSE is about 3.09 lakhs.

The R² score is 0.994, meaning the model explains about 99.4% of the variation in the test target.

## Limitations

- The dataset contains only 10 houses.
- Only three features are used: Area, Bedrooms, and Age.
- Real house prices depend on many other factors.
- The model may not perform the same way on a larger real-world dataset.
